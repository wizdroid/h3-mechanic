# 04 — Framing, angles and timing

Faces warp when they cover too few pixels or sit near the edges of a wide lens. These rules keep faces sharp and identity-true in H3.

## Shot sizes

| Size | Phrase | Faces readable? | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extreme close-up | `an extreme close-up of her eyes` | Partial | Inserts: eyes, lips, hands, a key |
| Close-up (CU) | `a close-up of <Subject 1>'s face` | **Best** | Emotion, reactions, single lines |
| Medium close-up (MCU) | `a medium close-up framing <Subject 1> from the chest up` | **Best** | Default for dialogue |
| Medium shot (MS) | `a medium shot framing both of them from the waist up` | Good, **max 2 faces** | Two-shots, walk-and-talk from the front, gestures |
| Medium-wide (MWS) | `a medium-wide shot framing them from the knees up` | Fair | Entrances, door actions, faces not the focus |
| Wide (WS) | `a wide shot` | Poor | Rear views, establishing, silhouettes only |
| Extreme wide (EWS) | `an extreme wide shot` | No | Establishing landscapes and buildings, tiny figures from behind |

## The face rules

1. **Dialogue and readable faces:** CU, MCU or MS only. The speaking face is `front-facing` or `three-quarter`, ideally one speaking face per shot. A two-shot with dialogue is MS with both faces three-quarter toward camera.
2. **Wide and extreme wide only** for: views from behind, establishing shots, silhouettes, or moments where faces do not matter. Never a front-facing wide on a speaking person.
3. **Lens look:** `shot on an 85mm lens` for CU/MCU, `50mm lens` for MS, plus `compressed perspective, natural proportions`. Do not write `24mm`, `wide-angle lens`, `fisheye`, `ultra-wide`, `GoPro` or `action cam` on a shot that shows faces.
4. **Placement:** subjects near the center of the frame or on the inner thirds, never at the frame edges, where distortion is worst.
5. **Distance:** `the camera keeps a steady distance` on every tracking shot with faces.
6. **Motion:** in wides keep camera speed slow; limit fast subject motion. Fast moves go on CU/MS only.
7. **Arrivals:** a person approaching the camera stops `in a clear medium framing with her face natural and undistorted`; they do not walk into the lens.
8. **People count:** at most two readable faces per shot. Extras are `softly out of focus in the background`.
9. **Resolution:** render faces at 2K when budget allows; 768P is fine for rear wides.
10. **Expression on every readable face** (see [06-expressions-and-delivery.md](06-expressions-and-delivery.md)).

## Angles

| Angle | Phrase | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| Eye level | `at eye level` | Default, neutral, most face-safe |
| Front-facing | `front-facing toward the camera` | The speaking face |
| Three-quarter | `in a three-quarter view, turned slightly toward the camera` | Two-shots, listening faces, most flattering |
| Profile (side) | `in clean side profile` | Silhouette against light, thinking, looking at someone off-frame. Keep at CU/MCU |
| Over-the-shoulder (OTS) | `over the shoulder of <Subject 2>, whose shoulder is softly blurred in the foreground` | Dialogue coverage; the far face is front-facing |
| Low angle | `from a slightly low angle` | Power, authority (an officer giving an order). Keep "slightly"; a strong low angle on a face distorts |
| High angle | `from a slightly high angle` | Vulnerability, guilt, a convict on the floor |
| Overhead / top-down | `from directly overhead` | Patterns, bodies on a bed or floor; no readable faces |
| Rear | `from behind` | Walk-aways, exits, escorts; the only angle where wide is free |
| Dutch | `a tilted Dutch-angle medium shot` | Unease, a mind cracking |

## Shot count and duration

| Duration | Shots | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 4–6 s | 1 (max 2) | One beat. One short line or a reaction |
| 7–10 s | 2 (max 3) | Action + reaction, or behind + front |
| 11–15 s | 2–3 (max 4) | Short exchange; each line needs its own shot |

- Silent insert ≥ **1.5 s**, action shot ≥ **2.5 s**, shot with a line ≥ **(line duration + 1 s)**.
- Line duration: English about **2.5 words per second**; Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Malayalam about **4–5 syllables per second**. Add **0.5 s** for a reaction after the line.
- The last shot runs to the end of the clip. The description must fill the whole duration with action; do not leave the final seconds undescribed.

## Timing math

Cut times are `MM:SS.mmm`, strictly increasing, and smaller than the duration. `[Shot 1]` has no timestamp.

Example, 10 s, three shots of 4 s / 3.5 s / 2.5 s:

```text
[Shot 1] …                                              (0.0–4.0 s)
[Shot 2] At 00:04.000, the camera cuts to …             (4.0–7.5 s)
[Shot 3] At 00:07.500, the camera cuts to …             (7.5–10.0 s)
```

Example, 15 s, two shots: back wide 6 s, front medium 9 s → `[Shot 2] At 00:06.000, …`.

## Coverage patterns

- **Two-shot escort (behind + front):** `[Shot 1]` wide from behind, tracking, no faces; `[Shot 2]` cut to MS from the front, tracking backward ahead of them at a steady distance.
- **Shot–reverse–shot:** speaker A MCU front-facing over B's shoulder; cut; speaker B MCU over A's shoulder. One line per shot.
- **Door / threshold:** start MS on the person at the door; the door opens; the person moves through and ends in MS on the far side. Shoot from inside the destination room so faces come toward camera.
- **Reaction insert:** a 2 s CU of the listener after a line lands.

## Phrase bank

- `framed at eye level in a medium close-up, front-facing, shot on an 85mm lens with compressed perspective and natural proportions`
- `centered in the frame, the camera keeps a steady distance`
- `their faces stay clear, natural and undistorted`
- `the background falls into soft, shallow depth of field`
- `seen from behind in a wide shot, their faces not visible`
