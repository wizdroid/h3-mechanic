# 06 — Expressions, emotions and vocal tones

Paste-ready snippets for `detailed_description` / `integrated_multimodal_description`. Write the face as physical, visible detail (eyes, brows, mouth, jaw, breath, shoulders), not as an abstract label. **One expression clause per readable face per shot**, and describe how it changes if it changes.

## Facial expressions

| Emotion | Snippet |
|---|---|
| Neutral, composed | `her face is calm and composed, her gaze steady, her lips relaxed` |
| Guilt | `she lowers her head, her eyes downcast and unable to meet his, her lips pressed together, her shoulders slightly hunched` |
| Shame | `she turns her face partly away, her cheeks tense, her eyes fixed on the floor, one hand gripping her sleeve` |
| Strict, stern | `his jaw is set, his brows drawn slightly together, his eyes hard and unblinking, his mouth a flat line` |
| Contempt | `one corner of his mouth lifts in a faint sneer, his eyelids half-lowered as he looks down at her` |
| Anger, controlled | `his nostrils flare, his jaw tightens, and a vein shows at his temple while his voice stays low` |
| Rage | `her eyes widen and blaze, her brows drop hard, her teeth show as she shouts` |
| Fear | `her eyes widen, her breathing turns quick and shallow, and she presses back against the wall` |
| Shock | `his eyes snap wide, his mouth falls slightly open, and he freezes mid-step` |
| Grief | `her face crumples, her chin trembles, and tears spill down her cheeks` |
| Holding back tears | `her eyes glisten, she swallows hard and blinks rapidly, forcing her lips into a tight line` |
| Relief | `her shoulders drop, she lets out a long breath, and a small, tired smile appears` |
| Respect | `she looks up at him with a quiet, steady gaze and gives a small, respectful nod` |
| Attraction, subtle | `her eyes linger on him a moment longer than needed, a small warm smile forms, and she lowers her gaze softly` |
| Shy | `she smiles with her lips closed, glances up through her lashes, then looks away` |
| Love, tender | `his eyes soften, a gentle smile spreads slowly, and he tilts his head toward her` |
| Joy | `her face breaks into a wide, open smile, her eyes crinkling at the corners` |
| Suspicion | `his eyes narrow slightly and flick from her face to her hands and back` |
| Defiance | `she lifts her chin, holds his stare without blinking, and her lips press into a hard line` |
| Exhaustion | `her eyelids are heavy, her face pale, and her shoulders sag as she leans on the bars` |
| Pleading | `her brows rise in the middle, her eyes wet and wide, her hands pressed together in front of her chest` |
| Menace | `he smiles slowly without it reaching his eyes, his head tilted slightly, watching her` |
| Regret | `he closes his eyes for a moment, exhales through his nose, and his mouth tightens` |
| Determination | `his gaze hardens and fixes ahead, his jaw squares, and he gives one short nod` |

### Emotion transitions (arcs inside one shot)

- `Her annoyance softens into hurt as her brows relax and her eyes drop.`
- `His stern expression flickers, a trace of pity crossing his face before his jaw sets again.`
- `Her guilty look hardens into defiance as she raises her eyes to meet his.`
- `The smile slowly drains from his face, replaced by cold suspicion.`

## Vocal tones and deliveries

Pattern: `<speaker> (Sx) <verb> in <voice phrase>, <delivery>, <d>[Language] …</d>`

| Delivery | Verb + delivery wording | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Whisper | `whispers … barely audible, leaning close` | `(S2) whispers in a soft, breathy young female voice, barely audible, <d>[Kannada] ನನ್ನನ್ನು ಕ್ಷಮಿಸಿ.</d>` |
| Shout | `shouts … at full volume, her voice cracking at the top` | `(S1) shouts in a hard, clipped mid-low male voice, at full volume, <d>[Kannada] ನಿಲ್ಲು!</d>` |
| Stern command | `orders firmly … in a low, controlled voice that leaves no room for argument` | `(S1) orders firmly in a deep, gravelly middle-aged male voice, <d>[Kannada] ಒಳಗೆ ಹೋಗು.</d>` |
| Guilty mumble | `mumbles … quietly, eyes down, the words trailing off` | `(S2) mumbles in a thin, fragile young female voice, quietly, the words trailing off, <d>[Kannada] ನಾನು ಏನೂ ಮಾಡಿಲ್ಲ.</d>` |
| Sob | `says through sobs …, her breath hitching between words` | `(S2) says through sobs, her breath hitching between words, <d>[Hindi] मुझे माफ़ कर दो.</d>` |
| Choked up | `says …, her voice catching and thick with tears` | |
| Pleading | `pleads … in a rising, desperate tone` | |
| Sarcastic | `says … with dry sarcasm, stretching the words and smirking` | `(S1) says with dry sarcasm, smirking, <d>[English] Oh, of course. You're completely innocent.</d>` |
| Tender | `says softly … in a tender, warm tone, almost smiling` | |
| Menacing | `says slowly … in a low, menacing tone, each word deliberate` | |
| Cold, flat | `says … in a cold, flat tone with no emotion` | |
| Deadpan comic | `says … completely deadpan, without a change of expression` | |
| Excited | `exclaims … quickly and breathlessly, bright with excitement` | |
| Breathless | `pants out the words … between heavy breaths` | |
| Nervous | `stammers … , repeating the first word` | |
| Laughing | `says … while laughing, the words bubbling out between laughs` | |
| Furious, contained | `says through clenched teeth … in a low, shaking voice` | |

| Voiceover | `says in an off-screen voiceover:` + `while her lips remain completely closed` after `</d>` | |

## Mouth discipline

- After the line: `She closes her lips and holds his gaze.` This stops lip movement continuing into silence.
- Listening faces: `his lips stay closed as he listens`.
- Non-verbal sounds attached to a face (a gasp, a sob, a sigh) can sit in the shot. Ambient and repeated non-verbal sounds also go in `overall_soundscape`.
