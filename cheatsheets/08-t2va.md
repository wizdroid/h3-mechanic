# 08 — T2VA (text-to-video with audio)

Source format: MiniMax H3 "Video Prompt Writing Guide (T2VA / I2VA / FL2VA / L2VA)".

Use when the user has **text only** — no reference or frame images.

## Procedure

1. Pull from the request: characters, setting, action beats, lines, duration, ratio. Default **8–10 s** and **16:9** when unstated. T2VA needs a **concrete ratio**; `adaptive` is rejected.
2. Because there are no references, describe each character once, clearly, at first appearance (age, build, skin tone, hair, clothing) and keep the same short handle afterwards (`the officer`, `the young woman`). You may add consistent scene, character, action and sound details.
3. Pick voices from the [voice library](05-voice-library.md) for each speaker.
4. Plan shots, sizes, angles, one move each, and cut times with the [framing rules](04-framing-and-timing.md) and [motion rules](03-camera-motions.md).
5. Write the three fields. The first sentence of `[Shot 1]` states the style and opening composition:
   `[Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a medium shot frames…`
6. Run the [pre-flight check](16-preflight-checklist.md), then output.

## Template

**No instruction line.** The prompt begins directly with the first field.

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, <opening composition>, <subjects and positions>, <environment and light>. <One camera move>. <Action, expressions, sounds>. The <voice description> (S1) says, <d>[Language] …</d> [Shot 2] At 00:05.000, the camera cuts to …

overall_soundscape: …

non_diegetic_music: …
```

- `integrated_multimodal_description` is **one continuous paragraph**; shots follow each other inline.
- Each field is written `field_name: content` on one line, with a blank line between fields.
- Common style words for the opening: `Live-action`, `cinematic`, `2D-animated`, `3D CG`, `claymation`, `watercolor`, `vintage film`.
- In T2VA the speaker is a description, not a label: `The stern officer with a deep, gravelly voice (S1) orders, <d>…</d>`

## Output

The prompt in one ```text fence, then: duration, resolution, ratio (concrete), voice IDs.

## Worked examples

### 1. Village courtyard at dusk: mother confronts her son (10 s, Kannada, static then push-in)

Voices: mother **F7** + rustic North Karnataka accent, son **M9**. Settings: 10 s, 2K, 16:9.

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a medium shot at eye level, shot on a 50mm lens with natural proportions, frames a stern Indian woman in her late fifties with grey hair in a low bun and a faded maroon cotton saree, standing in the doorway of a mud-walled village house at dusk. Warm orange light from an oil lamp behind her outlines her silhouette while the courtyard glows blue with evening. In front of her, a thin young man in his twenties in a creased white shirt stands with his back three-quarter to the camera, head bowed. The camera holds a static shot. Her jaw is set and her eyes hard as she looks down at him. The older woman with a rich, resonant voice and a slight gravel, speaking Kannada with a rustic North Karnataka accent (S1) says slowly and coldly, <d>[Kannada] ನಿನ್ನಿಂದ ಈ ಮನೆಗೆ ಅವಮಾನ ಆಯ್ತು.</d> She closes her lips and does not blink. [Shot 2] At 00:05.000, the camera cuts to a medium close-up of the young man, front-facing, shot on an 85mm lens, his face lit by the lamp light from the doorway. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward his face. His eyes glisten and drop to the ground, his chin trembles, and he swallows hard. The young man with a thin, tight voice that rushes and stumbles over the words (S2) pleads, his voice catching, <d>[Kannada] ಅಮ್ಮಾ, ನನ್ನನ್ನು ಕ್ಷಮಿಸು.</d> He closes his lips and presses the back of his hand to his mouth as the shot ends.

overall_soundscape: Crickets chirp across the dusk courtyard while a cow bell clinks faintly in the distance. The oil lamp flame flutters softly, and the young man's unsteady breathing is audible in the quiet.

non_diegetic_music: A slow, low shehnai phrase over a soft tanpura drone, fading out under the final line.
```

(`ನಿನ್ನಿಂದ ಈ ಮನೆಗೆ ಅವಮಾನ ಆಯ್ತು.` = "Because of you this house has been shamed." `ಅಮ್ಮಾ, ನನ್ನನ್ನು ಕ್ಷಮಿಸು.` = "Amma, forgive me.")

### 2. Night chase through a rain-soaked market: wide from behind, medium from the front (8 s, no dialogue)

Settings: 8 s, 2K, 21:9.

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a wide shot from behind at eye level shows a young Indian woman in a wet off-white cotton saree running away from the camera down a narrow, rain-soaked night market lane, her face not visible, past shuttered stalls and swinging bare bulbs. Twenty meters behind her, a uniformed police constable in khaki runs after her, one hand holding his cap. Rain streaks through the yellow bulb light and puddles splash under their feet. The camera tracks them from behind at slow speed at a steady distance, keeping both figures centered in the frame. [Shot 2] At 00:04.000, the camera cuts to a medium shot from the front, framing the woman from the waist up as she runs toward the camera, shot on a 50mm lens with compressed perspective and natural proportions. The camera tracks her from the front, moving backward ahead of her at the same pace and keeping a steady distance so her face stays clear and undistorted. Her wet hair clings to her cheeks, her eyes are wide with fear, her mouth open as she gasps for breath, and she glances back over her shoulder once before looking ahead again. The constable is a soft blur far behind her in the shallow depth of field.

overall_soundscape: Heavy rain hammers on tin awnings and splashes in puddles. Two sets of running footsteps slap on wet stone, and the woman's ragged breathing grows louder in the second shot, with a police whistle shrilling in the distance.

non_diegetic_music: Fast, driving taiko-style drums at a quick tempo with a pulsing low synth bass, building in volume until the end.
```

### 3. Voiceover memory: a woman at a cell window (6 s, English off-screen voiceover)

Voice: **F3** + light Indian English accent. Settings: 6 s, 2K, 16:9.

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Live-action, cinematic, a medium close-up in clean side profile at eye level, shot on an 85mm lens, frames an Indian woman in her early thirties with dark hair tied back loosely, wearing a plain off-white cotton prison saree, standing at a small barred window high in a grey cell wall. A single shaft of pale morning light falls across her face and the dust floating in the air. The camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward her face. Her eyes are calm but distant, and a faint, sad smile appears and fades. The woman (S1) says in an off-screen voiceover, in a warm, velvety female voice with a calm, even pace and a light Indian English accent: <d>[English] Every morning, the light finds me here. Like it has not given up on me.</d> while her lips remain completely closed. She closes her eyes slowly as the light brightens on her face.

overall_soundscape: Quiet cell room tone, distant birdsong outside the window, and the faint echo of a far-off metal door.

non_diegetic_music: A sparse solo piano at a slow tempo, single notes with long sustain, swelling slightly as the light brightens.
```
