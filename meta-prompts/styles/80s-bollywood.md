# H3 style — 80s Bollywood

1980s Hindi masala-film look: 35mm film grain, saturated Eastmancolor-style
palette, soft diffusion glow, star-filter highlights, hard studio key lights.

- Lighting: hard studio key lights, rim and kicker lights, soft diffusion glow.
- Color: saturated Eastmancolor-style palette, warm highlights.
- Lens/camera: zooms and crash zooms, static frontal compositions, low angles on
  the hero; star-filter highlights on lights.
- Costume/sets: period costumes and sets; theatrical dialogue delivery; dhishum
  fight sounds.
- Sound/music: synth-disco or string-sting scores.

Opening: `The target video is in a 1980s Hindi masala-film style with saturated
Eastmancolor color, 35mm film grain, and a soft diffusion glow.`
